## Web · party games

PlayPicka brings party games to the browser. Its first playable game, Which of Us / Qui de nous ?, starts with a choice of mode, then a phone passed between friends. Each round becomes a way to discover how the group sees its members.

## Contribution

I work on the game experience and its development, from choosing a mode to moving through the rounds. Which of Us is available now; the platform also introduces further games in preparation.
