## Godot · game project

Jack & Snag is a robot FPS in development with Godot. Armed combat, grappling and movement shape the action across stylised arenas, alongside editable systems for building levels.

## Contribution

I work on creative direction, level design and development, with AI assistance in implementation. The gallery shows real gameplay captured from the development build: armed robots on the glass track and a first-person view of the Frutiger Ball arena.
