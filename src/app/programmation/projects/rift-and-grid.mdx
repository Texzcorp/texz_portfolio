## Web · strategy game

A small tic-tac-toe board opens into a larger strategic space. Hidden, simultaneous choices change the familiar rhythm: each player commits a move before discovering the other player's decision.

## Contribution

I work on the design and development of this browser game. Local play, a bot and online rooms offer different ways to explore its rules, with a tutorial for the first game. Rift & Grid is free to play.
